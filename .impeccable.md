## Design Context

### Users

Developers, potential collaborators, recruiters, and open-source users visiting Yash Timbadiya's portfolio to understand his work, experience, craft, and reusable components quickly.

### Brand Personality

Precise, thoughtful, and quietly playful. The interface should communicate technical confidence, obsessive attention to detail, and care without feeling corporate or ornamental.

### Aesthetic Direction

A refined editorial and technical portfolio inspired by chanhdai.com, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Vercel, and Devouring Details. Use a monochrome Ink/Paper identity in light and dark modes, fine structural lines, generous rhythm, serif moments for personality, and rare spectral color accents. Avoid generic card grids, glassmorphism, glow-heavy dark UI, and decorative gradients without meaning.

### Design Principles

1. Preserve the grid: fine rules, shared alignment, and measured spacing should make each section feel engineered.
2. Make small details memorable: use one distinctive interaction or motion moment instead of animating everything.
3. Keep the identity monochrome; reserve color for intentional emphasis and discovery.
4. Keep typography expressive but legible, pairing restrained sans/mono utility text with editorial serif moments.
5. Design for both themes, mobile-first performance, keyboard access, and reduced-motion preferences.
